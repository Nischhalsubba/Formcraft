About Formcraft

A portfolio that explains how the work was shaped.

Formcraft is designed and developed by Nischhal Raj Subba as a living record of product work, design systems, experiments, and implementation decisions.

The final application may use a licensed CodeIgniter foundation, but its public information architecture, visual identity, interaction model, components, and content system are being rebuilt for this product.
